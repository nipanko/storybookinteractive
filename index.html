ELEMENTZ

The Chemistry & Elemental Adventure

Where Science Feels Like Magic

In the fractured universe of ELEMENTZ, natural elements are scattered across ruined temples and volatile biomes. As an Elemental Alchemist, you don't just solve puzzles—you conduct real chemical reactions. Combine hydrogen and oxygen to forge water, trigger exothermic detonations to shatter crystalline gates, conduct electricity through noble metals, and manipulate states of matter in real-time.

TARGET PLATFORMS
PC / Steam Mobile (iOS & Android) WebGL Demo

Interactive Periodic Fusion

Experiment with real elements. Mix alkali metals with moisture to ignite bursts, or synthesize acidic compounds to corrode obstacles.

Dynamic State Mechanics

Freeze flowing rivers into solid ice bridges, boil toxic swamps into harvestable gas, and manipulate phase shifts in real time.

Zero Rote Memorization

No boring flashcards. Atomic numbers, valencies, and bond types are discovered through puzzles that test intuition and creativity.
