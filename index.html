Our Mission

To make every learner the hero of their own story

🌟 Storybook Interactive is an independent game studio from India. We believe that learning should be an adventure, not a chore!

Our games are designed to make education engaging, interactive, and genuinely fun for learners of all ages. Through creativity and innovation, we're building interactive experiences that combine the joy of play with the power of learning.

Nipanko Nath Mazumder
